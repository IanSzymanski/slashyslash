How to Play

  • Shield soaks damage first and refills after 3 seconds without being hit. Tether shots spend 15 shield, so it is both armour and fuel.
  • When your shield breaks, the overflow is ignored and you get a moment of invulnerability.
  • A yellow ! means an attack is winding up. Tap sheathe as it lands to parry, then release for a counter that chains lightning to nearby enemies. Parried bolts fly back where you aim.
  • Holding sheathe longer charges the draw. Past about half charge, everything you cut freezes for a beat before it splits.
  • Plunge from higher up for a wider, harder shockwave. Slashing in the air keeps you aloft.
  • Tethered enemies take 25% more damage. Yank one into your combo, or zip in and land a tether strike. Brutes are too heavy to yank, so the tether pulls you instead.
  • Red barrels explode. Yank one into a crowd.
  • Kills charge the Sawblade. While it runs you cannot be hurt, the tether is free with no range limit, and slash lunges toward the cursor.
