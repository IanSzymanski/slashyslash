How to Play

  • Your shield (50) soaks damage first and refills after 3 seconds without being hit. The parry stance and each tether shot cost 15 shield, so it is armour and fuel at once.
  • When your shield breaks, the overflow is ignored and you get a moment of invulnerability.
  • A yellow ! means an attack is winding up. Press parry just before it lands. A successful parry refunds the shield, and releasing the key fires a counter that chains lightning to nearby enemies. Parried bolts fly back where you aim. A missed parry does nothing and the stance goes on cooldown.
  • Click during a dash, or right after one, for a dash slash along the cursor line.
  • Click while sliding to drag the blade through the street and spray rubble ahead of you.
  • Plunge from higher up for a wider, harder shockwave. Slashing in the air keeps you aloft.
  • Buildings are scenery: run through them and climb their roofs and balconies. Hold crouch on a platform to drop through it.
  • The tether never pulls enemies. Tap it to fling yourself at the target, hold it to zip in for a tether strike. It grabs barrels and pulse grenades instead: hold to swing them on the line, release to throw at the cursor. Tethered enemies take 25% more damage.
  • AEGIS-07 is shielded. It charges a pulse grenade (watch the arc it previews), lobs it, and the grenade detonates on a timer. Getting caught knocks your shield out for six seconds. Tether the grenade, swing it, and throw it back (or parry it) to break the rival's shield and stun it. The shield returns after ten seconds.
  • At half health AEGIS-07 drops its shield and guns for good and fights in blade combos. Parry one of its swings and it guards: your counter gets parried and it ripostes. Parry the riposte to stagger it.
  • Kills charge the Sawblade: invulnerable, free tether with no range limit, and slash lunges toward the cursor. Hold the tether on an enemy to grind it with damage that keeps climbing.
